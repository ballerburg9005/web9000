## Project purpose and strict invariants

WEB9000 is a **permissionless decentralized, censorship-resistant, server-replaceable content publisher**: start with an accessible retro-style seven-column keyboard-navigable image board to attract readers; develop into a serious text/idea/discussion/organizing/marketplace platform. Media is an attraction; discussion, context, and credible civic publishing must have first-class visual value.

- **Anyone may sign and publish to ANY public namespace**. No one can permanently reserve a community/space name by being first, spending tokens, or controlling a relay, host, domain, group, curator or database. Never reproduce Plebbit-like autocratic name-sniping or paid gatekeeper capture.
- **Signature validity is separate from presentation/replication.** No site, reviewer, group, DAO or stake service gets to pronounce an authentic outsider event globally invalid. Each willing peer and each reader independently chooses what to store, show, filter, quarantine and endorse.
- **No global deletion.** Existing signed events and their author-signed corrections/withdrawals remain portable and independently verifiable from willing holders. A peer may lawfully refuse local storage and remove its own copies; a signed post cannot be guaranteed retrievable if nobody preserves it.
- **Signed text/metadata and binary media are DIFFERENT replication layers.** Ordinary browsers by default keep only signed records/text, structured metadata and IPFS CIDs in reader-chosen scopes. **Do not automatically mirror/pin files**, make unexpected binary downloads, or enroll browsers as libp2p serving nodes. Images/audio/video/archives belong on voluntary specialized IPFS nodes or explicitly opted-in, disk-budgeted clients. A gateway URL advertised by a third party is a *suggestion*, not reader consent. Actual viewer fetching must be from explicitly approved gateways and should be byte-verified in the future. HTTP caching during intentional viewing differs from permanent replication.
- **Pilot media limits:** JPEG/PNG/WebP max **256 KiB** each; allowed audio MP3/Ogg/WAV and video MP4/WebM max **10 MiB = 10,485,760 bytes** each (binary units, NOT decimal MB). Media limit belongs to prototype ingest rules, not all possible future signed records. Operator storage cap, IP rate caps, safety review and quarantines remain their own policies.
- **Separate raw and qualified indexes.** Default curated/qualified presentation should not quietly elevate unknown unstaked/unchecked users. User can explicitly choose raw/unverified view. Stake4Karma locks (proposed around USD $10, with sufficient original/remaining lock time) are optional spam-defense inputs to voluntary qualified storage, **not permission to produce a signed record**. No real independent on-chain verifier exists yet; the currently empty qualified index and mock UI cannot be described as an economic security system.
- **No permanent infrastructure dependency.** OrbitDB indexes, gateways, domain hosts, DNS, bootstrap, WebSocket bridges, relays and moderators must remain replaceable. Peer discoverability, anti-entropy, selective subscription and redundancy need substantial implementation. Site owner preference is not public-namespace sovereignty.
- **Reader governance is voluntary, auditable, reversible and forkable.** Signed charters, member decisions, quorums, rival ratified branches, rules and reviewer labels are *claims* with evidence; readers explicitly select adopted groups/forks, retain raw audit view and appeals. Groups should be able to delegate specialization across bounded hierarchies, not suppress every outsider's posts or make their members' individual speech an automatic organizational act. Do not equate a report with wrongdoing.

## Owner's latest direction — governance, membership, karma, nested groups

**Latest explicit preference 2026-11-19:** karma should **primarily be required to INITIATE group votes/proposals**, together with a minimum amount of **approved group membership tenure**. A follower/subscriber is *NOT* an approved member; group member consensus, positive/negative member karma, or explicit admission votes (ideally displayed beneath relevant posts) decide approval under a **signed, adjustable group charter**. An approved member can still lack initiator eligibility until reputation/tenure thresholds mature. Every member's democratic vote matters; high karma need not simply multiply votes. An earlier speculative capped weighted second chamber was a possible design, **not owner sign-off**.

Many thresholds must scale with group size and observed group activity; no fixed global formula was approved. Inactivity must not automatically remove voting rights or permit hostile takeover of a quiet group. Timed ordinary For/Against decisions should finish without indefinitely requiring every dormant account, but constitutional membership/moderator/quorum changes must require stricter proof, notice and challenge. Multiple distinct signatures can be collected asynchronously. Signed member timestamps are self-declared; there is not yet independent time/finality consensus.

Nested groups should optionally share rulesets and delegate specific flagging, abuse screening and expert moderation services, with scoped authority, role expiry, evidence, appeal, cycle handling and local adoption. **No child group may become mandatory for publishing to a namespace.** Preserve ability to fork/disagree. Do not hard-code unproven group karma; no public social graph leakage by default.

**Current experiment:** `v2/core/governance.js` has signed founder charter, member proposals/votes, quorum parent snapshots, `timed-v1` 7-day **advisory** motions, 7-day binding info changes, 14-day higher-threshold constitutional updates, rival selectable ratified forks and portable branch inclusion proofs. Legacy charters preserve their older quorum policy. These durations are **provisional engineering defaults**, not approved final democratic rules. There is NO verified group-karma gate, no full multi-group specialist delegation and no trusted timestamp witness. See `docs/OPEN-ISSUES.md`.

## UI product requirements, current and future

**Default UI is `v2/index.html` (mirrors `v2/classic.html`) + `v2/classic.js` + `v2/classic.css`.** Seven-column responsive classic board, not `v2/app.js`/dashboard. Maintain focused old-web compactness, no giant cards/buttons, useful tag rows, image/video/audio and text-first modes, search/filter, polls, event schedules, jobs, offers, profiles, native comments, signed revision/retraction, group discovery. Text posts deserve extra vertical padding/visual salience; user wants meaningful reading, not only swipeable images.

When expanding a post: **media goes first, then title, provenance/author/date, votes/report/audit, tags, text, comments. No close button.** Click the selected tile or press Escape to close. Preserve scroll unless the selected row is significantly clipped; support arrow navigation and keyboard focus without replacing focused comment editor during network batches. Respect original gallery primary media and preview ordering. Attachment UI should not depend on JSON, IPFS knowledge, or user-supplied CIDs.

**Recent explicit buttons (owner correction 2026-11-19):** replace `Trust author` with `Add friend` / `Add foe` (currently reversible and *local only*; credibility effect deferred). **ZERO inline debug controls** in posts, revisions, reactions, tags or galleries: remove `Dump post`, `Dump comments`, `Copy signed record`, `Copy CID`, and `Inspect signed event` from article UI. **Debug is a pop-up opened solely from the top-right page HEADER beside Join Network.** Inside it: pick a locally signed post and use **Dump post / Dump comments**, inspect filtered signed records, live WebSocket traffic captured since startup **before** Debug opens and recent sessionStorage history across reload, paused view/refresh, copy/export, and local peer/IPFS/group/social diagnostics. ## Research direction and constraints

**Nostr research already completed** in `docs/NOSTR-RESEARCH.md` with primary NIP links. Highest-value patterns: **NIP-65 signed read/write relay lists and outbox routing; NIP-77 Negentropy set reconciliation; NIP-32 typed moderation labels/NIP-56 reports; NIP-42/98 scoped signed authentication; NIP-46 remote signing; NIP-b7 Blossom's signed media-server lists/content hash verification**. NIP-29 explicitly ties group access enforcement to a relay, though migration/forks are supported; **DO NOT import relay master-key community dictatorship or first-claimer ownership**. WEB9000 Ed25519 events and Nostr Schnorr secp256k1 events have different wire signatures; interoperability requires deliberate translation/dual-signing with provenance, not pretending they are compatible.

## Explicit anti-patterns

No fake claims of finished economics, transport, moderation or privacy. No global group veto, irrevocable ban/delete, single mandatory relay, forced stake for raw signed posts, auto-downloaded unknown media, default public personal social graph, mock score elevated to signed authority, hidden historical votes, censorship-by-specialist-without-appeal, unspecified numerical karma formulas hard-coded as policy, or treating label count as verified wrongdoing. No replacing classic image-first design with a generic analytics UI.
