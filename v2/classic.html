Profile metadata is signed by your key and can be replicated by willing peers. Anyone can publish a name; it does not claim a namespace or prove real-world identity.

Your public key signs posts, replies and group votes. It belongs to you, not to a namespace owner or test peer. The encrypted backup carries the signing key only; it does not archive all posts and attachments.

Public identity

Keep your backup and password separately. Only enter the password on websites whose code you trust: scripts served by a compromised host can access browser signing keys. Restoring a different key does not rename your previous posts.
Numeric karma test provider

Simulation only. Assign a numerical score to a public signing identity to prototype how qualified feeds might behave. This does not represent locked coins, independently earned reputation, or an on-chain verification. This browser stores its own simulated numbers.

Score 10+ passes the local simulated feed. The shared qualified peer remains independently verified and does not accept these numbers. Do not mistake this for Sybil protection.
